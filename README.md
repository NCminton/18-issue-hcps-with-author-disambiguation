This study is based on large-scale bibliometric data primarily obtained from the Essential Science Indicators (ESI) module of Web of Science (WoS) and the open-access platform OpenAlex. HCPs identified by ESI have become an important bibliometric indicator of high-impact scientific output and have been widely used for research performance analysis at different levels, including individual researchers, research institutions, and countries, as well as for assessing disciplinary development trends and research competitiveness. ESI updates its list of HCPs, every two months, resulting in six updates per year. Based on ESI, we collected HCP data for Physics, Biology & Biochemistry, and Computer Science across 18 issues, comprising six issues in 2019, six in 2020, and six in 2021. For each HCP, the collected information included the Web of Science accession number, unique article identifier, PMID, title, authors (AU), publication journal, volume and issue, page range, publication date, ESI field, citation count (TC), author countries (CT), author addresses, author institutions (C3), and publication year.
